Mercor RLS Admin Tools helps authorized users inspect, export and administer Mercor Studio tasks. This policy describes the extension's handling of information. Access to Studio remains subject to the organization's sign-in and access requirements.

Information the extension handles

To provide its tools, the extension may access the current Studio page URL; account, company, campaign, task and world identifiers; user names, email addresses and user identifiers; task contents, review and transition histories, feedback, statuses, assignments and requested file contents. It reads limited page information needed to identify the current context and display available actions. It does not collect a general browsing history.

Authenticated requests use the existing signed-in Studio page session. The extension does not ask you to enter your Okta password. Session tokens are used for authorized requests within the Studio page and are not returned to the extension, stored in its settings, or included in its logs or exports.

When you enable Paste Assistance, it handles your paste events and the paste keyboard shortcut on that Studio document. Pasted text is inserted into the selected editable field. The extension does not keep or upload a separate clipboard log or continuously read the system clipboard. Studio may save text entered into its fields as part of its normal operation.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to retrieve and display Studio records, produce requested exports and downloads, perform confirmed administration actions, maintain configuration, and recover interrupted operations.

The extension communicates over HTTPS with Mercor Studio and the exact file-delivery origins you approve for requested downloads. Those services receive the requests needed to provide the chosen feature. Studio authentication headers and cookies are not forwarded by the extension's file-download helper to file-delivery services.

The extension has no separate developer-operated data-collection server, advertising or analytics integration. We do not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated purposes. User data is handled only for the extension's disclosed features, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

If you choose to send support correspondence or exported diagnostics, the recipient receives the information you include. The extension does not send diagnostics automatically. We use information voluntarily sent to our support contact to respond to the request and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations.

Local storage and retention

Chrome extension storage keeps settings, campaign/status mappings, approved delivery origins, interruption markers and operation-recovery records. Recovery records can contain account, user, campaign, task and world identifiers, intended assignments and statuses, operation progress and hashes. They do not contain session tokens, downloaded file bodies or complete world-flow snapshots. Unfinished records may remain until recovery is completed and cleanup is verified.

Results and diagnostics are generally held temporarily in memory. Files you explicitly download or export remain wherever you save them until you delete them. The extension does not use Chrome storage sync to synchronize its data across devices.

Your controls

You can disable Paste Assistance or fully reload the page to remove it from that document. You can revoke Studio site access, disable the extension, or remove it from Chrome. Settings provides controls for clearing/resetting configuration. Removing the extension removes its own local Chrome extension storage; downloaded files must be deleted separately.

Complete any pending operation recovery before removing local recovery information. Clearing local extension data or uninstalling does not delete or undo records and changes already stored in Studio. Contact the organization that operates your Studio account for requests concerning those server-side records.

Other services, updates and contact

Mercor Studio, the organization's identity provider, file-delivery services, Chrome and the Chrome Web Store operate under their own applicable policies. Visiting this public policy page may also provide ordinary web-request information to its hosting provider; the supplied policy page contains no added analytics.
